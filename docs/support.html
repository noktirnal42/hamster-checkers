Before Filing

  • Update to the newest TestFlight or App Store build.
  • For Online 2P, confirm Game Center is signed in on each device with different Apple IDs.
  • Try Refresh in the Online 2P panel if a turn or active match does not appear immediately.
  • Attach a screenshot or short screen recording when it helps explain the problem.

Private account credentials, payment information, and personal contact details should not be posted in public issues.
